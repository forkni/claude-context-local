## Gate evaluation

Per the plan's pre-registered criteria (Step 4):

- **Adopt** a cap-relief default only if, on **both** 63q and 133q, the `w50`-vs-`w15` CI on
  `recall@10` **or** `recall@20` excludes zero on the upside.
  → **NOT MET.** Zero of the four relevant CIs (63q recall@10, 63q recall@20, 133q recall@10, 133q
  recall@20) excludes zero on the upside. Closest: 63q recall@20 upper bound +0.0003, 133q
  recall@20 upper bound +0.0020 — both still include zero.
- **Guard-rail — MRR CI must not exclude zero on the loss side on either set**: holds. 63q
  `[−0.0270, +0.0063]`, 133q `[−0.0211, +0.0324]` — neither excludes zero on the negative side.
- **Guard-rail — recall@5 (named)**: holds, flat on both sets (63q Δ −0.0058, 133q Δ +0.0004, both
  CIs wide and centered near zero).
- **Guard-rail — unexplained |latency Δ| > 100ms/query**: **breached on 133q.** `w50` vs `w15`
  latency Δ = +310.9ms/query (4633→4703ms is +69.5ms on 63q, under threshold; 5438→5749ms is
  +310.9ms on 133q, over it). Directionally consistent with the intended mechanism — `w50` widens
  gate-2's raw candidate pool 20→100 before gate 4's score-sort trims it back to 30, so more
  candidates require similarity scoring even though the final admitted count is unchanged — not
  treated as an anomaly, but named per the guard-rail's own text since it exceeds the stated bound.
- **Close the seam permanently** if `w50`-vs-`w15` point estimates are non-positive on `recall@10`
  **and** `recall@20` on **both** sets: **narrowly not triggered, on a noise-level technicality.**
  133q satisfies it (recall@10 Δ −0.0032, recall@20 Δ −0.0375, both negative). 63q's recall@20 point
  estimate is negative (−0.0343) but recall@10's is nominally positive (+0.0078) — however this
  estimate sits deep inside a CI that spans −0.0185 to +0.0348 (n_moved=10/63, SE=0.0139), i.e. it
  is indistinguishable from zero and not a real positive signal. The literal trigger text requires
  strict non-positivity, so this doc records the trigger as **not formally met**, while noting the
  substantive evidence (no CI excludes zero anywhere in either direction, recall@20 the only metric
  with a consistently negative point estimate on both sets) supports the same practical conclusion
  as a permanent close.

**Disposition**: **REJECTED for adoption.** No default change. Given the near-miss on the literal
permanent-close trigger, this is recorded as a full rejection rather than a formal permanent close —
but the evidence gives no basis to expect a positive result from either reordering lever (QW1,
A2′), both of which are strictly bounded above by this ceiling and were already independently
screened out at Step 2 (QW1's `R2` policy: 0/0 newly-admitted addressable golds on both datasets,
attributed to global PageRank centrality not tracking per-query relevance — the same finding this
repo has replicated before, `centrality_alpha 0.0`). Treat this seam as **effectively exhausted**
for practical purposes; `ego_graph.max_neighbors_per_hop` is locked in
`FORBIDDEN_AUTO_TUNE_KEYS` regardless.

**Reopening condition**: a design that changes what gate 4 selects on, not just how many candidates
survive gate 2 to reach it (e.g. a genuine anchor-conditioned relevance score at the truncation
boundary, replacing global centrality or raw discovery order) — the same condition already recorded
for the neighboring `graph_hop_window_cap` seam. Do not re-propose a plain cap raise or reorder
without that.

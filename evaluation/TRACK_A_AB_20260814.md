## A2 — confidence-weighted traversal

| Arm | 63q | 133q |
|---|---|---|
| `traversal_confidence_weighting_enabled=true` | **byte-identical** (0 movers) | **byte-identical** (0 movers) |
| `min_traversal_confidence=0.6` | **byte-identical** (0 movers) | **byte-identical** (0 movers) |

**Gate: N/A — treatment has no effect at any shipped-relevant setting.** Structural explanation (confirmed empirically):

1. **Weighting is inert at max_depth=1**: `get_neighbors` returns a neighbor *set*; edge weight only orders the BFS priority queue. With one hop there is no traversal-order-dependent truncation, so ordering cannot change membership.
2. **Floor 0.6 drops nothing**: resolver-injected edges are already floored at injection time (`call_graph.min_confidence=0.65`), and in-file AST edges carry no float `resolver_confidence` attr → `_edge_confidence()` defaults them to 1.0. Nothing in the graph lives in (0, 0.6).
3. First floor that can bite is **0.8** (drops pyan-0.75 and AST-0.7-tier resolver edges). Diagnostic arm `a2f08_63q`: MRR −0.0040 [−0.0322, +0.0243], recall@10 −0.0026, recall@20 +0.0013, pool_hit 0; only 2 MRR movers (Q12 1.0→0.25 — known boundary-riding fusion-cut query — vs Q77 0.5→1.0). Quality-neutral trade; no adoption case.

**Disposition**: mechanism stays shipped-disabled at defaults (0.0 / False — byte-identical, unit-tested). Reopening condition: multi-hop depth >1 traversal (where ordering gates membership), or an injection-floor redesign that admits sub-0.65 edges the traversal floor would then have something to filter.

## Verdict: keep defaults (k1=1.5, b=0.75)

- No grid point improves MRR and R@5 together on either set. The trade-offs are consistent
  in direction but small: `k1=1.2` gains R@5 (+0.016..0.024) at MRR cost (−0.008..−0.019);
  `k1=2.0, b=0.75` gains MRR (+0.009..0.011, under the ±0.02 floor) with flat recall;
  `b=0.4` gains Hit@5 but collapses MRR (−0.08) on both sets — poison for RRF rank fusion.
- Since no candidate passes the BM25-standalone primary gate, the fused no-regression run
  is unnecessary. k1/b are saturated at the library defaults for this corpus — do not
  re-sweep without a corpus change (e.g. after Track D).
- Q102's gold rides length normalization (rank 15 at b=1.0, 39 at b=0.4) but Q103/Q122
  golds sit at ranks ≈1,500–1,700 regardless of scoring params: they need *content* signal
  (Track D path/symbol tokens), not scoring-curve changes.
